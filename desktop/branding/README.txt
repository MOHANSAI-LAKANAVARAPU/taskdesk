Hi-Focus TaskDesk mascot icons

hifocus-mascot.svg       Full mascot on the light-blue circle (app icon, floating icon, dashboard)
hifocus-mascot-nobg.svg  Mascot without the circle
hifocus-tray.svg         Simplified shield face for small sizes (tray icon, favicon)
icon-512.png / icon-256.png   App and installer icons
tray.png (32px), tray@2x.png (64px), tray-16.png (16px)   Tray icons

The mascot SVGs have named groups for animation:
  #eyes      blink (scaleY)
  #arm-wave  wave (rotate around the shoulder, about x=404 y=128)
  #body      bounce
